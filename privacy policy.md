 1. Introduction

Welcome to SKYGLOW.

SKYGLOW is a casual bubble shooter game developed for Android devices.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, processed, stored, and used when you play SKYGLOW.

We aim to respect your privacy and minimize the information processed by the game.

 2. Information Stored Locally

SKYGLOW does not require users to create an account.

Game progress, scores, high scores, and game settings are stored locally on your device.

SKYGLOW does not operate its own cloud save system, gameplay analytics service, or progress server.

Local game data is not automatically synchronized between devices.

Uninstalling the application or clearing its storage may delete locally stored game progress.

 3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

SKYGLOW uses Google Mobile Ads SDK through the AdMob integration.

The application may display:

- Banner advertisements.
- Rewarded advertisements.

Advertising services may process certain information, which may include:

- Advertising identifiers.
- Device information.
- IP address.
- Advertising interactions.
- Diagnostic and performance information.

The specific information processed depends on the SDK configuration, device settings, consent status, and applicable Google policies.

Google may use this information to provide, measure, and improve advertising services, prevent fraud, and comply with legal requirements.

SKYGLOW does not operate its own advertising data server.

For additional information, please review Google's privacy information:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

 4. Advertising Consent

Where required by applicable law and Google policies, advertising consent choices are presented through an appropriate consent mechanism.

Users may be able to manage certain advertising and privacy preferences through their device settings and available consent options.

The availability and type of advertisements may depend on consent status, location, and network connectivity.

 5. Children's Privacy

SKYGLOW's intended audience is 6+.

The application is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Advertising configuration and audience settings will follow applicable Google Play Families requirements and relevant privacy laws where applicable.

 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress and settings remain stored locally on the device until they are deleted by the user, the application is uninstalled, or the device's application data is cleared.

Advertising-related information may be retained and processed by Google and its advertising services according to their own policies and retention practices.

SKYGLOW does not maintain its own central database of player accounts or gameplay progress.

 7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the application and its locally stored information.

However, no digital storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
